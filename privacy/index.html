JobRush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026
Applies to: the JobRush browser extension ("JobRush – Automatic Job Finder & Auto Apply").
Contact: open an issue on GitHub

The short version

  • JobRush has no account and no JobRush database. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Everything JobRush knows about you stays in your browser (Chrome's extension storage on your device) and on your own Notion board.
  • The AI runs on your device: open-source models that download once to your browser and run there. Your resume and answers are not sent to an AI company.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for anything other than finding and applying for jobs for you.
  • The only server JobRush runs is a small Notion sign-in helper (jobrush-notion.psberkeleymba.workers.dev). It swaps Notion's one-time sign-in code for an access token and sends it straight back to your browser. It stores nothing: no database, and no logs of codes or tokens.

What JobRush handles, and where it lives

Information Why JobRush needs it Where it is kept
Your resume and the profile read from it (name, contact details, work history, education, skills) To fill job applications for you Your browser's extension storage
Your saved answers (for example work authorization, sponsorship, minimum salary, locations) To answer the fixed questions on application forms exactly as you set them; these are never guessed Your browser's extension storage
Your job-search rules and their change history To decide which roles to look for and apply to Your browser's extension storage
Your LinkedIn connections file (optional, you upload it) To show which companies you know people at Your browser's extension storage; company-level counts are written to your board
Roles found, applications filled and sent, and activity To track your job hunt Your browser's extension storage and your own Notion board
Notion access token (or a Notion integration key, if you choose to paste one) To create and update your board in your workspace Your browser's extension storage
The pages of job sites you apply on To read and fill the application form Read in your browser while the form is open; not stored except the outcome (sent, needs you, sign-in needed)

JobRush does not collect browsing history, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or location. It does not read pages other than job listings, application forms, your board and (only if you start a LinkedIn search) LinkedIn job pages in your own signed-in browser.

Who JobRush sends information to

JobRush only sends information where you point it:

  1. The employer's application form, when an application is submitted. By default JobRush shows you each filled application and waits for you to press Submit; you can turn on automatic submission in Rules. What is sent is what you saved in JobRush.
  2. Notion (api.notion.com): company cards, roles and statuses, written to your workspace with the access you granted. Notion's own privacy policy applies to data in your workspace.
  3. Job sources (public company career pages and job boards, and LinkedIn job search in your own browser): JobRush requests job listings from them, the same way your browser does when you visit them.
  4. Model downloads: the first time you turn on private AI, the browser downloads open-source model files from a public model host. These are downloads only; none of your information is sent.
  5. The Notion sign-in helper (jobrush-notion.psberkeleymba.workers.dev, a Cloudflare Worker run by JobRush): receives Notion's one-time sign-in code, exchanges it with Notion for your access token using JobRush's integration secret, and returns the token to your browser. It does not store the code, the token or anything about you, and it is not used for anything else.

Nothing else is sent to JobRush. There is no JobRush analytics or tracking service.

If you use Copy for an AI agent in the Rules tab, JobRush copies your rules and your sponsorship and work-authorization answers to your clipboard (never your phone, email or address). Where you paste them is up to you, and that service's privacy policy applies.

Google Sheets as a board is coming soon and is not available yet. This policy will be updated before it is.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain words, for data JobRush handles:

  • It is used only to provide JobRush's single purpose: finding job openings that match your rules, filling and submitting applications you allow, and tracking them on your board.
  • It is not transferred to anyone except as described above, to provide that purpose, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • It is not used or transferred for advertising, including personalized ads.
  • It is not sold, and not used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No person reads it. JobRush has no staff access to your data because JobRush never receives it.

Your choices and control

  • See and change everything: your answers and rules are in JobRush's Rules tab; changes made by chatting are shown before they apply and can be undone.
  • Choose how fresh: you pick how recent roles must be (the last 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days or 30 days; 7 days by default).
  • Pause at any time with the Start/Pause button.
  • Disconnect Notion at any time (Notion > Settings > Connections).
  • Delete: removing the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser. Your board stays in your Notion workspace, which you control and can delete there.

Children

JobRush is a job-search tool for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Security

Data stays on your device and in services you already use. Access tokens are kept in extension storage, which other websites cannot read.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. A material change to how data is used will be shown in the extension before it takes effect.
