JobRush

Fresh jobs. Applied for you. JobRush finds newly posted roles that fit you, fills the applications and tracks every company on your own Notion board.

Add to Chrome — it's free

No account. Private AI on your device.

Fresh roles, found for you

Checks company career pages and job boards for new roles that match your rules. You choose how fresh: 24 hours to 30 days, 7 days by default.

Applications filled for you

Fills each form with your saved details. It shows you each one first; turn on auto-submit if you want, with a daily limit.

Tracked on your Notion board

One card per company, from New Roles to Interviews, with the people you know there. Google Sheets is coming soon.

Set up in one message

Upload your resume and tell JobRush what you want in one message. Change your rules later by chatting, with Confirm and Undo.

Private by design

No account and no JobRush database. Your data stays in your browser and your own board. The AI runs on your device.

Safe by default

Never creates accounts or types passwords. Sponsorship and work authorization are answered only as you saved them, never guessed.
